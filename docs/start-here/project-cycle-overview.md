# Project Cycle Overview

This page describes the full Lunabotics project cycle from kickoff through post-competition handoff.

## 1. Team Onboarding

Capture:

- New member training plan.
- Required safety training.
- Tool access and account setup.
- CAD, code, electrical, manufacturing, and project management onboarding.
- Recommended first tasks for new members.

## 2. Requirements and Rules Review

Capture:

- Competition rule changes.
- Robot constraints.
- Scoring priorities.
- Field or environmental assumptions.
- Questions sent to competition organizers.
- Requirements that affect design decisions.

## 3. Concept Development

Capture:

- Brainstormed concepts.
- Selection criteria.
- Trade studies.
- Rejected ideas and why they were rejected.
- Early sketches, prototypes, or simulations.

## 4. Detailed Design

Capture:

- CAD architecture.
- Major subsystem interfaces.
- Electrical architecture.
- Software architecture.
- Manufacturing plan.
- Bill of materials and vendor decisions.

## 5. Build and Integration

Capture:

- Assembly order.
- Fit issues.
- Wiring changes.
- Integration blockers.
- Parts that were difficult to manufacture or assemble.
- Tools, fixtures, and jigs used.

## 6. Testing and Iteration

Capture:

- Test plans.
- Test setup photos.
- Data collected.
- Failures and root causes.
- Design changes made because of testing.
- Tests that should be repeated next year.

## 7. Competition Preparation

Capture:

- Packing list.
- Pit setup.
- Spare parts.
- Driver/operator training.
- Software deployment checklist.
- Known risks.
- Emergency repair procedures.

## 8. Competition and Postmortem

Capture:

- Match/run results.
- What broke.
- What surprised the team.
- What procedures worked.
- What should change next year.
- Final recommendations.
