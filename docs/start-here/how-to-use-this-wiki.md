# How to Use This Wiki

This wiki is for preserving team knowledge across years. It should answer questions like:

- What did we do!
- Why did we do it that way?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What would we change next time?
- Who should a future team member talk to or learn from?

## Writing Guidelines

Write for a future teammate who is smart, busy, and missing context.

Good pages include:

- A short summary at the top.
- Clear ownership: who wrote it and what role they had.
- Process steps, not just final outcomes.
- Links to CAD, code, tests, photos, meeting notes, spreadsheets, or other source material.
- Lessons learned and warnings.
- Open questions for next year.

Avoid:

- Pages that only say "we should document this later."
- Decisions with no explanation.
- Uploading files with no context.
- Assuming next year's team knows team slang, vendor names, part numbers, or past design history.

## Recommended Update Rhythm

- Weekly: add short notes on progress, blockers, and decisions.
- After major design reviews: document decisions and tradeoffs.
- After tests: record setup, results, failures, and next steps.
- Before competition: document operating procedures and known risks.
- After competition: write honest retrospectives.
- End of year: complete handoff pages for each role and subsystem.
